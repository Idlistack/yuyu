Some organisations share their event photos through People's Gallery. If yours does, you can add an optional selfie so People's Gallery can find the photos you appear in and tell you when they're ready. You add it in Yuyu; only People's Gallery keeps it, for matching.

## Adding a selfie

You need a Yuyu account and a confirmed registration. If you registered as a guest, create an account with the same email address first, then open your ticket while signed in.

<Steps>

1. Open your ticket. After you register, the event page also shows an **Add an optional selfie** link.
2. Under **Find your photos**, choose **Add a selfie**.
3. Read the notice. It explains what the selfie is for, who sees the results and how long it's kept.
4. Tick **Match me in this event's photos** and **I am 18 or older**, then choose **Agree and continue**.
5. Choose **Open camera** and **Take photo**, or **Choose a photo**. Use a photo of just you, facing the camera, in good light, without sunglasses.
6. Check the preview and choose **Use this photo**.

</Steps>

You'll see **Selfie added** when People's Gallery accepts it. If it can't use the photo (no face found, more than one face, too small, too dark or blurry), the step says why: try another photo.

<Callout kind="note" title="Where your selfie goes">

Your phone or browser sends the selfie straight to People's Gallery. Yuyu never receives or keeps a copy. People's Gallery uses it only to match you in that event's photos, and deletes it with your matches 180 days after the event ends, or sooner if you stop face matching. Only you see the photos matched to you.

</Callout>

The selfie step is optional. Your registration and ticket work exactly the same without it.

## Updating or stopping

- To use a different photo, choose **Update selfie** on your ticket. The new one replaces the old one.
- To stop, choose **Stop face matching** on your ticket. People's Gallery deletes your selfie and the photos matched to you for that event. You can add a selfie again later.
- **Account** → **Privacy** lists every event where you used face matching. **Stop face matching everywhere** stops it for all of them at once.

If you stop face matching inside People's Gallery instead, Yuyu shows the change here too.

## Under 18

Face matching is only for people aged 18 or older. If you're younger, choose **I'm under 18** in the notice. Face matching then stays off for that registration, and nothing else about your registration changes.

## "Your photos are ready" emails

When People's Gallery has photos of you, Yuyu emails you on its behalf, for example "Your 23 photos from Tech4Good Summit are ready", with a link to see them. People's Gallery never gets your email address.

To stop these emails, go to **Account** → **Privacy** and turn off **Email me when People's Gallery has photos of me**. You won't get them either once you stop face matching or cancel your registration.

## Good to know

- You don't see the selfie step for a registration that is waitlisted or waiting for approval, for a recurring series, or once the event's photos are no longer kept.
- Organisers can see that you added a selfie, but not the selfie itself.
- See [Your ticket](/help/attending/your-ticket) and [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data).

Your account settings hold your name and photo, how Yuyu looks for you, and the controls that keep your account safe. Open your account menu (your name or picture in the sidebar) and choose **Account settings**.

The page has five tabs: **Profile**, **Registrations**, **Organisations**, **Security** and **Privacy**. The header above them shows whether your email is verified, whether two-step verification is on, and when you joined. [Your data and memberships](/help/account/your-data) covers the Registrations and Organisations tabs, and [Finding your photos with a selfie](/help/attending/find-your-photos) covers the Privacy tab.

## Your profile

The **Profile** card sets how you appear across Yuyu, for example to your organisation's other members.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. To use your own photo, choose **Choose profile image** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB. Use **Replace image** or **Remove** to change it later.
3. Edit your **Display name** (up to 120 characters).
4. Choose **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Without an uploaded photo, Yuyu uses the picture from your Google account if you signed in with Google, then your Gravatar if you have one, and otherwise your initials.

## Email address

The **Email** card shows the address Yuyu sends your tickets and account emails to, and whether it is **Verified**. If it says **Not verified**, choose **Resend verification email** and follow the link. You need a verified email to create events.

You can't change your email address yourself yet.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** in the **Appearance** card. **System** follows your device's setting. The choice is saved in this browser only, so set it again on other devices.

## Password

Open the **Security** tab.

- **Change your password**: in **Password**, enter your **Current password**, a **New password** of at least 12 characters, and **Confirm new password**, then choose **Update password**.
- **Add a password**: if you've only ever signed in with Google, the card is called **Add a password**. Enter and confirm a new password and choose **Add a password** to be able to sign in with email and password too.

Either way, you're signed out on every device, including the Yuyu mobile app, and sign in again with the new password.

<Callout kind="note">

Password changes need a recent sign-in. If Yuyu says "Sign in again before changing your password" (or, for Google accounts, "Sign in with Google again before adding a password"), sign out, sign back in, and make the change within ten minutes.

</Callout>

Forgotten your current password? Use [Forgotten your password?](/help/account/forgotten-password) instead.

## Two-step verification

The **Two-step verification** card shows **On** or **Off** and lets you set up or turn off an authenticator app. See [Two-step verification](/help/account/two-step-verification).

## Sign-in methods

**Sign-in methods** lists the ways into your account: **Email and password** (shown as **Set** or **Not set**) and any linked account such as **Google** (shown as **Connected**). A linked account's own security settings apply when you use it.

## Recent security activity

This card lists your ten most recent security changes with the time of each:

- Two-step verification turned on or off
- Password added, changed, or reset by email link
- Signed out everywhere
- Allowed an app to sign in with Yuyu, or disconnected one

If you see a change you didn't make, change your password and choose **Revoke all sessions** straight away, then turn on two-step verification.

## Connected apps

Some apps let you sign in with your Yuyu account instead of creating a new one, for example **People's Gallery** for event photos. The first time, Yuyu shows what the app wants, such as "Know who you are" and "See events you're part of", and you choose **Allow** or **Cancel**. After you allow it once, Yuyu doesn't ask again for that app.

An app you connect sees your name and profile photo. It never sees your email address or your password.

**Connected apps** on the **Security** tab lists the apps you've allowed and when. To stop an app using your Yuyu account:

<Steps>

1. On the **Security** tab, find the app under **Connected apps** and choose **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm with **Disconnect**.

</Steps>

The app can no longer use your Yuyu sign-in, and it signs you out the next time it checks with Yuyu. If you sign in to it with Yuyu again, it asks for your permission again.

Changing or resetting your password, turning two-step verification on or off, and **Sign out everywhere** also end your sign-in to connected apps in the same way. The apps stay connected, so signing in again doesn't ask for permission.

## Sign out everywhere

Lost a phone, or signed in on a shared computer? **Sign out everywhere** ends every session at once.

<Steps>

1. On the **Security** tab, choose **Revoke all sessions**.
2. Confirm with **Revoke sessions**.

</Steps>

Every browser and device, including the Yuyu mobile app and the one you're using, is signed out immediately, and connected apps lose your Yuyu sign-in. Like other security changes, this needs a sign-in within the last ten minutes.
